11. ASSIGNMENT ENGINE TECHNICAL DESIGN
Primary Algorithm:
Hybrid Mode.

Step 1
Receive Lead.

Step 2
Determine Source.
Example:
1|FACEBOOK

Step 3
Find Route.
Example:
1|FACEBOOK↓Telesales Team

Step 4
Load Active Agents.
Filter:
active = trueavailability = available

Step 5
Rank Agents.
Sort by:
lowest leads today↓oldest assignment↓highest priority

Step 6
Select Agent.

Step 7
Create Assignment Log.

Step 8
Update Bitrix Lead.

Step 9
Create CRM Activity.

Step 10
Send Notifications.
